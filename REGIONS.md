# Arcade areas

The playlist is `game-config.json` → `endless.regions`. Areas join without ending the run. Existing pursuers stay alive across boundaries. The infinite slope is now called **Arcade**; its internal `endless` keys and distance records are retained.

The first **450 metres use the original gentle opening**. Then the normal sequence is **town → quiet → tree corridors → lake → quiet → cemetery → dense forest → regular forest → wild patch → quiet**, repeating. Wide crowd encounters are inserted randomly farther downhill without skipping the next normal area.

| Config kind | Base length | Core challenge | Gates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `crowd` | 320–400 m | Rush Hour Slope: a broad, busy field with skiers across every lane, hunting Yetis and tree banks along both edges. Appears randomly later. | Normal slalom |
| `quiet` | 180–250 m | Snowy Trail: continues the actual town-exit snow road, with scattered nearby trees, a few walkers, slow skiers, a bench/lamp and snowman. Sparse woods continue off the path. | None |
| `trees` | 900–1,200 m | Existing long bends, forks, alternating gates and dense tree banks. | Weaving gates |
| `lake` | 240–330 m | Huge clear ice: set a heading, coast, then regain control on the quiet snow afterward. | None |
| `town` | 900–1,200 m | Actual Arcade-style village, narrowing street, pedestrians and back route. | Entry/exit only |
| `cemetery` | 650–850 m | Open graveyard slalom between irregular grave/crypt clusters. Several gaps stay available; resting zombies stir by graves, while extra walkers already patrol the gaps. Skeletons rattle, rise and leap once. Downhill movement is 70% of normal. | Entry/exit only |
| `denseForest` | 110–160 m | A short thick stand of trees with a narrow winding passage. Concentrate on threading through or jumping. | None |
| `scramble` | 70–110 m | Wild Patch: short randomized scatter of several tree species, rocks, stumps, bushes, sleds, small jumps and skier/boarder traffic. Find a gap or jump; no reserved centre corridor. | None |
| `forest` | 300–450 m | Ordinary sparse forest, slalom, traffic, smaller random lakes and occasional hills/rainbows. | Normal slalom |

`regions.minimumWidthMetres` is **600**. Cemeteries, towns, tree corridors, dense forest, Wild Patch, crowd fields and clear lakes keep their theme across at least that full width, scaled with entry pace. Cemetery flanks contain additional irregular burial clusters, monuments, mausoleums and sparse resting skeletons; town flanks contain irregular chalet blocks using the native row spacing. Forest and Wild Patch flanks retain their own obstacle types. Broad entry/exit tree wings or graveyard fences make a quick detour around the section harder, while the intended openings stay clear. These are physical obstacles, not invisible boundaries. Only cemetery boundary wings remain; the interior has no rail maze or stone dividers. Low fences and forest banks are jumpable. Ordinary sparse woods remain beyond the distant edges. Quiet trails and ordinary open forest keep their relief layouts.

Lengths scale with entry pace. They stay fixed for a visit, as does that visit's difficulty. Repeated crowds increase population, cemetery cluster spacing tightens42→34 m, its preferred clear gaps narrow18→12 m and more zombies appear, dense forests narrow their clear passage and increase tree density 1→2×, and regular forest density rises. Town and corridor progression retain their existing settings. Snowy trails and clear lakes remain relief; trail traffic stays light rather than increasing with visits.

`regions.order` can reorder or repeat any kind. `denseForestDensity` [1,2] sets first/final-visit tree density for Close Pines; the original first-visit geometry stays intact. `scrambleDensity` [1,1.35] separately controls Wild Patch scatter. Both freeze difficulty within a section and grow between visits. Wild Patch and Close Pines are separate kinds with independent visit counts. After a town, Snowy Trail extends that town’s existing approach road along the skier’s approach, then gently rejoins the course centre. Its width stays at Arcade’s 64 units, including later visits; it does not narrow with the town. Only its immediate path strip is cleared, so leaving it does not expose a large empty region. `regions.lengthMetres` controls the new area lengths; `endless.encounters.lengthMetres` still controls towns and long tree corridors. `regions.gapMetres` can add ordinary connector terrain. `regions.enabled: false` restores the preceding town/tree schedule.

`regions.crowd` controls random crowds: first eligible after 1,200 m, 55% chance at a safe transition, at least 1,600 m after the previous crowd ends, and forced at the next eligible transition after 4,200 m. Eligible transitions follow quiet/open forest, so crowds do not interrupt ice exits. Set chance to 0 to disable random crowds. Width is the larger of `crowd.widthMetres` (240) and `minimumWidthMetres` (600), scaled with entry pace; the slope is centred on the approach, populated out to both edges and bounded by dense tree banks. The configured 180→280 people are the total field population, capped at 600; increasing themed width no longer multiplies that count. The broad field and side banks remain, with more room between skiers. `regions.lengthMetres.crowd` controls length. The old `beginner`/`beginnerPeople` settings remain accepted as legacy explicit-layout options. `quietTreesPer100Metres` is 8 and `forestTreesPer100Metres` is [12,26]. `cemeteryOpeningMetres` is [12,18] in late/first order, matching the existing opening configuration convention. `zombies.count` is [8,14] in the central cemetery, with sparse additional skeletons beside streamed outer clusters (zero disables both), `speedKmh` is [7,11], and `noticeMetres` is 22. Ambush range/reach use the common pace scaling and the cemetery Y multiplier applies to leap motion. Skeletons leap once and then remain stationary; spent bodies use normal scenery cleanup rather than persisting as distant pursuers. Separate `wandererCount: [24,48]` and `wandererSpeedKmh: [2,3.5]` add slow green/brown shamblers, with four upright native pixel designs. These walkers rest beside individual graves, visibly stir when approached, then slowly follow the player through whichever gap is used. Additional `roamerCount: [12,24]` walkers patrol from the start. Setting the wanderer count to zero disables this role independently. Living predators do not treat undead as edible prey.

There are no new predator placements in quiet areas, clear lakes, dense forests, Wild Patches, cemeteries or towns; authored undead are the cemetery threat. Existing enemies can follow through. Resting skeletons are fully visible piles of bones, then rattle/rise before one committed leap; the first hit bumps an upright skier. The same attacker cannot immediately eat its own victim, but another skeleton, zombie or Yeti can catch the fallen skier. Regular green/brown zombies rest sitting beside their graves, get up over the windup interval and shamble toward the player; grounded contact is lethal. Shared jumping/sword/ram rules still apply. Zombie walkers have four green/brown designs; only jumpers use the exposed bone skeleton design. Static art and rest/rise/leap poses are cached.

Player-initiated rams require actual swept body contact, near-full speed for the current steering angle, and genuine approach/closing motion above `physics.minimumCollisionKmh` (8). The shared `wildlife.ramSpeedFraction` is .85 of attainable sliding/traverse speed for the current heading/mode; fixed species gates no longer override it. Perfect centre aim is not required, and collision checks keep the player's original impact motion even if another collision has just knocked them down in the same frame. A matched-speed touch or a pursuer catching the player from behind does not kill the enemy. Previously fallen players do not acquire a ram attack. Sleeping/waking/waiting monsters remain collidable, including fast glancing contacts between samples. Slower solid hits on those bodies crash the skier instead of only reducing speed. Jumps still clear them. Old species speed and bear/Yeti aiming-cone settings remain accepted for config compatibility.

No saved record version was reset for this expansion. Wallet, settings and the current Endless best stay intact.

## Possible additions

These are suggestions, not active area types:

- **Cemetery bell gates:** a shortcut through a narrow opening rings a bell and wakes nearby graves; a longer open route avoids the alarm. Suggested mechanic only, not active.
- **Wind pass:** gentle alternating gusts push the skier sideways through otherwise open terrain; anticipation matters more than obstacle density.
- **Jump meadow:** optional chains of hills offer faster routes, while occasional gate placements reward staying grounded and choosing a line.
- **Frozen river:** narrow snow crossings and short slippery ice shortcuts create a choice between precise steering and committing to an angle.

Towns each have one cocoa shop, claimed once at its front window for one shield and a cozy card dismissed by tap/key. The simulation and pursuers pause during the card, then resume at the same position. Normal shields fill the current run inventory; overflow/free-play gifts enter a saved cocoa-shield reserve. Predator deaths also show funny aftermath cards dismissed by tap/key, with attacker-matched Yeti art, before returning to the summit.

All killable enemies share `resolveEnemyContact`: successful rams kill the creature **and crash the skier**, using normal stopped-motion/fall/recovery behavior with no new immunity or equipment consumption.

Wide flank scenery streams in nearby world chunks, unloads beyond the surrounding window and regenerates with stable geometry when revisited. Revealing/leaping zombies detach from chunk cleanup, then become stationary and age out as scenery; their spawn IDs prevent duplicate live guards on re-entry. Hunters pursue directly when targets use the outer terrain instead of returning to a central route. Distant horizontal scenery is culled before render-layer sorting. Arcade towns, other region roads/gates and the gentle opening retain their settings.

Cemeteries use `regions.cemetery`: `ySpeedScale: 0.7` blends over 25 base metres at the boundary; X steering and clocks remain responsive. This applies locally to the skier, traffic, animals, pursuers and moving hazards, including jumps. `clusterSpacingMetres: [34,42]` is late/first spacing, before entry pace scaling. Rotated, jittered family clusters mix six gravestone styles with small crypts. A loose, unpainted generation line guarantees a fair gap, while several other openings remain available. There is no fenced main street, no internal stone wall and no forced sideways switchback. Hunters pursue directly in the open field. Entry/exit fences have broad 220-world-unit half openings and the usual checkpoints. The full 650–850-base-metre span remains; dropping the detours and restoring speed makes it a brief skiing section again.

`regions.zombies.ambush` retains the24 m skeleton trigger,0.6 s rise/rattle warning,0.65 s base leap,50 m nominal reach before Y projection and60-world-unit arc. The7→11 speedKmh values shorten later leaps; each leap aims once, so changing direction can dodge. Wanderers use noticeMetres22 and the same visible rise interval before slow pursuit. Every initial actor owns a separate nearby grave; resting spots are checked against nearby props and actors to avoid embedding bodies in a burial cluster. Jumping, sword saves and genuine physical rams retain shared behavior; a ram kills the monster and crashes the skier.

The former `cemetery.rows`, `crossingMetres` and `chokeWidthMetres` are accepted only for older JSON; the open layout no longer uses them. They are omitted from the published configuration. `cemeteryOpeningMetres: [12,18]` now reserves preferred clear gaps between clusters, not the width of a fenced path. Adjacent gaps can also be used, and moving zombies can obstruct either choice.

Number keys 1–9 jump to debug areas at each theme’s remembered visit: forest, tree corridors, town, crowd, quiet trail, cemetery, lake, dense forest, Wild Patch. Available at the summit or within Endless; press an arrow to ski from the entrance. Debug distance is excluded from records. See [README.md](README.md) for the key table.

With an area selected, `[` / `-` previews its previous visit and `]` / `+` its next visit (default range 1–6). The same preview seed and normal starting pace make the real per-theme progression easier to compare. The step is remembered per area during the session; relief areas keep their existing unscaled design.

`zombies.roamerCount: [12,24]` adds active patrols in the clear openings without replacing the resting zombies. One third of initial placements prioritize the course centre; the rest cover alternate gaps near the clear generation line, with prop/body clearance checked before spawning. `downedSpeedMultiplier: 2` applies only to alert walking zombies within noticeMetres of a player whose fall timer is positive. Resting/rising warnings and skeleton leap timing stay unchanged. When the skier is back up, even while awaiting fresh input, walkers return to normal speed and continue pursuing. Roamers are visible threats to other NPCs before player aggro.

Player-facing **Arcade** now means the infinite mixed slope (internal ID/config key `endless`, with existing distance records retained). The former stage campaign entrance is removed. Legacy choice IDs `arcade` redirect to `endless` and duplicate entrances are removed during config loading.
